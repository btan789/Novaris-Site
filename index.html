About Novaris.

Novaris is a student engineering team from Trinity Grammar School in Kew, Melbourne, competing in STEM Racing (formerly F1 in Schools). Teams design, manufacture and race miniature CO₂-powered Formula 1-style cars, and present their engineering and enterprise work to judges. Our car, NV-45 Redshift, won the Fastest Car award at the 2026 STEM Racing World Finals in Singapore.

At the World Finals in October 2026, Redshift took the Fastest Car award with a time of 1.068 seconds, and Novaris placed 14th of 91 teams overall on 729.6 points (official World Finals results). At the 2026 Australian National Finals, Novaris won the Development Class, taking the Knockout Racing and Grand Prix Racing titles and the award for Best Manufactured Car.
